# Next backlog (prioritised)

Ordered by what most limits learning from the first private sessions. Each item is a proposal for the creator to approve.

1. **Run the private sessions** with the [test kit](PLAYTEST_KIT.md) (5 cold testers first, then 10 to 20 strategy players). No
   other item replaces this: understanding a loss, seeing another plan and choosing to replay are unobserved.
2. **AI thinking time in battle**: a budget (time or orders searched) for the slice AI's decisions, which take up to about
   18 s in the worst positions measured; decide from whether testers find the waits tolerable.
3. **Economy tuning from session data**: yields against upkeep, repair and commission costs, so budget choices still bind after
   the first month (currently they do not). Use the metrics file and the ledger, not guesses.
4. **Readability pass** on the mandate, treasury, operation and doctrine windows (hierarchy, shorter lines, icons for costs),
   driven by where testers hesitated.
5. **Ground unit art** for the Pygmaei and Inhumanus planetary classes (or approved tokens), so planetary matches read clearly.
6. **Rematch balance**: whether an undamaged defender holding the centre should keep winning rematches on the round limit
   (objective weighting, contract timing or deployment depth), decided from human play.
7. **The rival launching operations** (it currently defends, raids, contests and negotiates).
8. **Fleets joining an operation in progress** as declared reserves.
9. **Strategic fog of war** (only if testers ask for scouting decisions); supply and the rival are built to read known facts only.
10. Later decisions kept out of this phase: a Silex pilot for defensive counterplay and the content pipeline; a token-based
   physical orbital test from the ship manifest; broader factions; ranked networking; public distribution.
